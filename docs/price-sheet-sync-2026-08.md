## Open questions

**1. The Eversys tariff, and whether the April list already contains it.**
Every machine on the April 2026 sheet is between +3.98% and +4.04% above the
previous list — a uniform ~4% move that matches the 4% tariff figure exactly.
Either Eversys folded the tariff into list pricing, or it is additive and every
machine needs another 4%. Prices are currently set to list exactly as printed,
which is defensible either way. **Confirm with Eversys before deciding**;
if additive, it is a one-pass update.

**2. The Legacy+ SKUs look transposed on the sheet.**
The sheet gives L'2c → `SE-L2M` and L'2m → `SE-L2C`. Given `c` = coffee-only and
`m` = milk everywhere else in the range, these appear swapped. They have been
entered verbatim as printed rather than silently corrected. **Confirm with
Eversys before the first Legacy+ order goes in.**

**3. Vitrifrigo FG20 capacity.** Listed as 2 Gallon on the Legacy+ and Cameo
pages and 4 Gallon on the Enigma and Shotmaster pages — same part number
(A—FG20), same 2,340. The listing notes the discrepancy.

**4. Shotmaster large drip tray is priced "$460/$570"** with no indication of
which configurations take the higher figure. Listed at 460 with a note.

**5. Enigma E'4m/ST moved +3.54%, not +4.0%** (41,530 → 43,000; a straight 4%
would be 43,191). The only machine off the uniform increase — possibly rounding,
possibly a typo. Entered as printed.

**6. The LM4 sheet is marked CONFIDENTIAL — DO NOT DISTRIBUTE** and its first
price column is partner/dealer cost. The store uses the Minimum Advertised Price
column throughout and must continue to; dealer cost must never reach the
storefront, a metafield, or product copy.

**7. La Marzocco e-commerce permission.** Note 4 on the sheet: *"Selling La
Marzocco & Modbar via E-Commerce requires the express written permission of
LMUSA."* The site has a live Shopify cart and hosted checkout. Worth confirming
that permission is on file before the LM listings transact.

**8. Two duplicate parts, one live at $0.00.**
`La Marzocco LCD Display GB5 — Part # E.1.004` (vendor "Vera Coffee Solutions")
is **ACTIVE at $0.00** and duplicates `E.1.004 — LCD display GB5 (La Marzocco)`
at 187.65. Similarly `La Marzocco Thermostatic/Mix Valve Treated — Part #
B.2.003` duplicates `B.2.003 — Thermostatic/mix valve treated`. Neither
duplicate is in a site collection so neither is currently reachable on the
storefront, but the $0.00 one should be archived. Left unchanged pending
your call.

**9. Swift and Swan grinders** are on the sheet (7,050 / 4,700) and already
priced correctly, but sit as drafts with no photography, so they do not appear
on the site.

**10. Modbar.** The sheet prices only one configuration (2 tap Espresso AV + 1
tap Steam, 26,630 — matches the store) and directs to a sales rep for the rest.
The store carries six further configurations that could not be verified against
any sheet.

**11. Mahlkönig naming.** The sheet calls them E80W and E65W Grind-by-Sync;
the store calls them E80S GbW and E65S GbW. Prices match, names do not.

## Note on site visibility

A product appears on the site only if it belongs to `machines`, `grinders`,
`parts-accessories` or `la-marzocco-parts` (see `CATEGORY_COLLECTIONS` in
`src/lib/shopify.ts`). Legacy+ was added to Machines; Eversys accessories and
options to Parts & Accessories; Mazzer to Grinders (drafts, so not yet live).
Pages revalidate hourly.
